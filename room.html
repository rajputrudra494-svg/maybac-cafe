The room upstairs.

Arches, globe lights, wine leather and walnut. Third floor, International Wealth Centre — take the lift and follow the smell of butter.

01 — How it came together

We wanted a room that feels the way dessert looks — dusty-rose arches, wine leather, warm walnut, and a pastry counter you’ll pretend not to stare at.

Maybac sits on the third floor of International Wealth Centre on VIP Road. The bar runs the length of the room under a canopy of scalloped arches, each one holding a small globe light. The chairs are deep wine leather, the tables are walnut, and past the glass there’s a terrace lined with palms for when the evening cools down.

The kitchen is entirely vegetarian and goes from avocado toast to butterfly-pea sushi. The pâtisserie is where the day really happens: New York cookies, Magnum croissants, tres leches four ways… and the bear.

02 — Have a look around

Pull up a chair.

A long bar under the rose canopy, a glass pastry counter, two rows of wine-leather tables and a terrace past the windows. Tap any photo to see it larger.

i.

The canopy

Scalloped arches in dusty rose run the length of the bar, each with its own globe light. It’s the first thing you see when the lift opens.

ii.

The counter

A lit glass case of whatever the pâtisserie made that day — cheesecakes, tres leches, croissants, cookies the size of your palm.

iii.

The ceiling

Painted panels and sculpted tiles overhead, so look up between bites. A white dancer sculpture stands among the palms by the window.
