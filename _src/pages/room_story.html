01 — How it came together

We wanted a room that feels the way dessert looks — dusty-rose arches, wine leather, warm walnut, and a pastry counter you’ll pretend not to stare at.

Maybac sits on the third floor of International Wealth Centre on VIP Road. The bar runs the length of the room under a canopy of scalloped arches, each one holding a small globe light. The chairs are deep wine leather, the tables are walnut, and past the glass there’s a terrace lined with palms for when the evening cools down.

The kitchen is entirely vegetarian and goes from avocado toast to butterfly-pea sushi. The pâtisserie is where the day really happens: New York cookies, Magnum croissants, tres leches four ways… and the bear.
