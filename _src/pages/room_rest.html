02 — Have a look around

Pull up a chair.

A long bar under the rose canopy, a glass pastry counter, two rows of wine-leather tables and a terrace past the windows. Tap any photo to see it larger.

i.

The canopy

Scalloped arches in dusty rose run the length of the bar, each with its own globe light. It’s the first thing you see when the lift opens.

ii.

The counter

A lit glass case of whatever the pâtisserie made that day — cheesecakes, tres leches, croissants, cookies the size of your palm.

iii.

The ceiling

Painted panels and sculpted tiles overhead, so look up between bites. A white dancer sculpture stands among the palms by the window.
