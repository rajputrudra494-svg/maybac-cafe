Good to know

Before you come.

Is everything vegetarian?

Yes, the whole kitchen is vegetarian. Dishes marked with a J on the menu can be made Jain, and we’re happy to talk through anything else.

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome. For evenings, weekends or groups it’s worth sending us a message first so we can hold a good table.

How late can I come in?

We’re open till 1 AM every day. The pastry counter thins out as the night goes on, so come earlier if you have your heart set on something.

Can you host a birthday or a small party?

We do, often. Décor, a dessert table and a set menu can all be arranged — give us a call a few days ahead.

Can I order to take away?

Yes. Call ahead and we’ll have it boxed, or find us on the usual delivery apps.
