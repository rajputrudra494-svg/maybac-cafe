01 — Upstairs at IWC

A rose-pink room with arches for a ceiling, wine leather chairs and a pastry counter that hums from late morning till well past midnight.

Coffee is pulled properly here — pour-overs, Aeropress, cold brew aged in barrels. The kitchen is fully vegetarian and wanders happily from burrata pizza to butterfly-pea sushi. And the pâtisserie, well. You’ll see.

The Sleeping Teddy.

Sleeping Teddy Mousse

Fluffy, dreamy, chocolatey — like a hug from a teddy bear. Made of 54% chocolate & cream.

It starts as a bear.

Chocolate mousse, set in a teddy mould and dusted in shavings so he looks properly furry.

Then, the blanket.

Warm chocolate goes over his back at the table, slowly, until he’s tucked in.

iii.
